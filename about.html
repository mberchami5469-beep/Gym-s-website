ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Berchami Academy is a boxing academy dedicated to developing athletes through discipline, hard work and professional coaching.

We offer group and private coaching for different levels, from beginners to experienced fighters.

With extensive experience in the field and a record of achievements at regional and national level, we are committed to helping every athlete improve their skills and reach their goals.
